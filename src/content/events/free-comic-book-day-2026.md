# Free Comic Book Day 2026

Join us for the biggest day in comics! **Free Comic Book Day** is an annual event where participating comic book shops give away free comics to anyone who visits.

## What to Expect

- **Free Comics**: Choose from a selection of specially printed FCBD comics
- **Special Deals**: Discounts on back issues and merchandise
- **Community Fun**: Meet fellow fans and celebrate comics together
- **Surprises**: You never know what we might have planned!

## Hours

We'll be open extended hours for this special day. Check back closer to the date for exact times.
